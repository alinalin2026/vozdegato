# Voz de Gato — Design Philosophy

## Chosen Approach: **Warm Advocacy**

A compassionate, accessible NGO design that prioritizes clarity and emotional connection. This approach emphasizes the wellbeing of cats through warm typography, generous whitespace, and high-contrast text that serves audiences aged 55+.

---

## Design Movement

**Humanitarian Design + Accessible Modernism**

Rooted in NGO communication best practices: clear messaging, emotional authenticity, and accessibility-first layout. The design avoids corporate coldness and instead conveys genuine care through warm colors, readable typography, and direct language.

---

## Core Principles

1. **Clarity Above All** — Every word, image, and button serves the mission. No decorative clutter.
2. **Warmth & Trust** — Warm earth tones (oranges, browns) paired with soft whites create a safe, welcoming space.
3. **Accessibility First** — Large, bold typography; high contrast; simple navigation; designed for 55+ readers.
4. **Emotional Authenticity** — Real cat photos, genuine language, no corporate jargon.

---

## Color Philosophy

**Primary Brand Color: Warm Orange (#FF8C00)**
- Represents energy, care, and the vitality of healthy cats.
- Used for CTAs, highlights, and the logo.

**Supporting Palette:**
- **Off-White (#F8F6F1)** — Warm, soft background; reduces eye strain.
- **Deep Brown (#3D2817)** — Text and strong accents; represents stability and earth.
- **Soft Gray (#A39E95)** — Secondary text and subtle dividers.
- **Accent Green (#6B8E23)** — Growth, life, healing (used sparingly).

**Emotional Intent:** Warmth + Trust + Care. The palette avoids clinical blues and instead uses earth tones that feel human and approachable.

---

## Layout Paradigm

**Vertical Flow with Breathing Room**

- Hero section with large, bold headline and real cat photo.
- Mission statement in a single, centered column (max-width: 65ch for readability).
- Alternating image-text blocks (image left, text right; then reverse) to create visual rhythm.
- Generous padding and whitespace between sections.
- Sticky header with logo and simple navigation.
- Footer with all contact info, organized clearly.

**No grid clutter.** Each section breathes independently.

---

## Signature Elements

1. **Paw Print Accent** — Small paw icon used as a bullet point or section divider.
2. **Warm Photo Frames** — Real cat photos with soft shadows and warm borders.
3. **Bold Typography Hierarchy** — Large, sans-serif headlines paired with readable body text.

---

## Interaction Philosophy

**Minimal, Purposeful Motion**

- Buttons respond with a subtle scale and color shift on hover.
- Links underline on hover (clear, accessible).
- Smooth scroll behavior (no jarring jumps).
- Contact form inputs have warm focus states (orange border).
- No autoplay videos or distracting animations.

---

## Animation

**Subtle, Respectful Motion**

- Button hover: `scale(1.05)` with 150ms ease-out.
- Link hover: underline fade-in, 100ms.
- Section entrance: fade-in on scroll (if JS available), 300ms.
- Form focus: border color shift to orange, 150ms.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: disable all animations for users who request it.

---

## Typography System

**Font Pairing:**
- **Headlines:** Poppins Bold (700) — Modern, friendly, readable.
- **Body Text:** Inter Regular (400) — Clean, accessible, proven for long reads.
- **Accent Text:** Poppins SemiBold (600) — Used for CTAs and emphasis.

**Hierarchy:**
- **H1 (Hero):** 48px / 56px line-height, bold, warm brown.
- **H2 (Section):** 32px / 40px line-height, bold, warm brown.
- **H3 (Subsection):** 24px / 32px line-height, semibold, warm brown.
- **Body:** 18px / 28px line-height, regular, deep brown (high contrast for 55+).
- **Small Text (Footer):** 14px / 20px line-height, regular, soft gray.

**Contrast:** All text meets WCAG AAA standards (7:1 or higher).

## Brand Essence

**One-line positioning:**
Voz de Gato speaks for cats who cannot speak for themselves—ensuring they are safe, fed, and protected.

**Personality Adjectives:**
1. **Compassionate** — We genuinely care.
2. **Clear** — We communicate directly, no corporate speak.
3. **Trustworthy** — Founded in 2024, grounded in real action.

---

## Brand Voice

**How headlines, CTAs, and microcopy sound:**

- **Authentic, direct, warm, never patronizing.**
- Avoid: "Welcome to our website," "Get started today," "Learn more."
- Embrace: Action-oriented, specific, cat-focused language.

**Example Headlines:**
- "Los gatos merecen protección. Nosotros hablamos por ellos." *(Cats deserve protection. We speak for them.)*
- "Seguridad, alimento, amor. Cada gato cuenta." *(Safety, food, love. Every cat counts.)*

**Example CTAs:**
- "Únete a nuestra misión" *(Join our mission)*
- "Dona ahora" *(Donate now)*
- "Contacta con nosotros" *(Contact us)*

---

## Wordmark & Logo

**Logo Concept:**
A stylized cat head in profile, with a megaphone or speech bubble emerging from its mouth. The cat is rendered in warm orange (#FF8C00), with the megaphone in a deeper orange or rust tone. Below, the wordmark "Voz de gato" in Poppins Bold, with a small tagline "Hablamos por ellos" *(We speak for them)* in smaller text.

**Logo Usage:**
- Header: 60px height.
- Favicon: Simplified cat silhouette, 32px.
- Footer: 40px height.

## Signature Brand Color

**Warm Orange: #FF8C00**

This is the unmistakable color of Voz de gato. It appears in:
- Logo and wordmark.

## Additional Notes

- **Imagery:** All photos are real cats in shelter environments, healthy and calm. No stock photos of sad or sick animals.
- **Language:** Spanish (Spain) throughout. Formal but warm tone.
- **Accessibility:** Large text, high contrast, keyboard navigation, clear focus states.
- **Mobile-First:** Responsive design that works beautifully on small screens.
- **Footer:** Complete contact info, social links (if applicable), and compliance info.
